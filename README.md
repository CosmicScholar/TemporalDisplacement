How the mechanism works
-Prediction creates credibility: 
early prophetic details come true.

-Credibility becomes presumed authority: 
the younger group mistakes accurate forecasting for endorsement.

-Hidden passages challenge that authority:
new evidence reveals that the prophecy describes an evaluation, 
not a coronation.

- A warning delivered through time makes the stakes explicit.

- Hubris determines the outcome: the younger group
  rejects correction and triggers the consequence

the younger group will do without approving of it. 
Being foretold is not the same as being chosen

being granted permission.
